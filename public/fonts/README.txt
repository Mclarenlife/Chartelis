Noto Sans SC, licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
Original: NotoSansSC[wght].ttf
Downloaded: 2026-10-12

NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf and NotoSansSC-Bold.ttf are static instances of the
original variable font, generated with fontTools 4.66.1 at wght=400 and 700.
No character subset was removed. These fonts are bundled for offline export.
KaTeX fonts are supplied by the katex dependency under its bundled license.

PDF export embeds complete CJK fonts: fontkit's CJK subset output can lose
visible glyphs in PDF readers even when text extraction appears correct.
Keep the render-and-text-extraction regression checks when changing fonts.
